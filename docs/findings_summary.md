# Findings Summary

## Key Findings

- Specialist booking SLA compliance was 94.29%, with 33 out of 35 specialist bookings completed within 48 hours.
- VOR SLA breaches were driven by delay reasons such as parts backorder, garage capacity, and incorrect job scoping.
- Some vehicles had downtime significantly above the fleet average, suggesting targeted review opportunities.
- Garage-level analysis showed variation in average VOR days and repair start delays.

## Operational Recommendations

- Review specialist bookings that exceeded the 48-hour SLA.
- Monitor garages with consistently higher average VOR days.
- Investigate parts backorder cases above fleet average downtime.
- Track repair start delays separately from total VOR to distinguish garage intake delay from repair duration.
